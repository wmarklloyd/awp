# Model-assisted design reviews

These documents preserve critiques produced with language models during AWP’s design. They are informative research inputs, not normative specification text, empirical validation, independent implementation reports, or peer review.

Filenames have been normalized for archival clarity. The contents retain their original reasoning and should be evaluated as unverified analysis. Accepted ideas are reflected separately in specifications, decision records, schemas, or conformance evidence.

Future reviews should record the model and version when known, date, prompt or review question, exact specification revision, available context, and whether a human verified each finding.
